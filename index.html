The Chronicle

Two hearts, one dua answered

Every imperial story begins quietly, and ours began with a dua whispered on a winter night, over cups of saffron chai and a book of poetry neither of us returned on time. One conversation that outlasted the Isha prayer, one shared shawl against the December cold — and somehow, that evening decided the rest of our lives.

Six years and three cities later, our families gathered beneath one roof to give their blessing, and the date was written with dates and water — the sunnah way. Nothing would mean more than to have you standing beside us as we say Qubool Hai, and begin a home built on sakinah, mawaddah and mercy.
